FAQ

Good questions.

Do guests have to pay?

No. It’s free play for every guest. You only pay for the rental. For markets and pop-ups we can set it up as pay-to-play instead.

What’s the difference between library and custom designs?

Library designs come from our collection of ready-made stickers, picked to match your theme. Custom designs are drawn just for your event, like your names, your pet or an inside joke.

How much space does it need?

About 2 × 2 feet. It doesn’t need an outlet, and it works best on a flat floor away from doorways.

Can it go outside?

Yes, as long as it’s under a tent or covered patio. Paper stickers and rain don’t mix, so we can’t set up in open weather.

How far ahead should I book?

Book at least 3 weeks out so we have time to design and print your stickers. We take one event per day, so popular dates go fast.

Do you stay during the event?

No. We set everything up before guests arrive and come back to pack up when it’s over. If you’d like someone there the whole time, add an attendant for an hourly fee.

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere in Metro Detroit. Delivery within 25 miles is included, and we travel farther for a small mileage fee.
